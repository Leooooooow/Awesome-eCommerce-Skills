# Creator Analysis

## What this skill solves

Turn creator signals into a clear fit decision and next step.

## Use when

- The user wants to evaluate a creator for a partnership
- The user wants to compare creators or shortlist candidates
- The user needs a prioritize / test / monitor / skip recommendation
- The user wants to understand creator fit, trust signals, or risk

## Do not use when

- The user only wants follower counts or a surface profile scan
- The user wants outreach copy instead of fit analysis
- The user wants campaign attribution or post-campaign reporting
- There is too little evidence to assess fit at all

## Inputs to ask for

Ask only for the minimum needed:
1. Creator handle or profile link
2. Platform and niche
3. Product or offer
4. Target customer
5. Collaboration goal
6. Any available content samples or metrics

## Workflow

1. Define the decision.
2. Normalize the context.
3. Evaluate audience relevance, content clarity, trust signals, conversion potential, and risk.
4. State the best use case for the creator.
5. End with a clear recommendation and confidence note.

## Output requirements

Always include:
- One-line verdict
- Strengths
- Risks or gaps
- Best collaboration use case
- Next action
- Confidence note

## Quality standards

- Prefer observable evidence over hype
- Separate reach from fit
- Separate polished content from conversion relevance
- Say when evidence is thin

## Safety / constraints

- Do not overstate fit without evidence
- Do not confuse beauty of content with business usefulness
- Do not turn this into contract or legal review
