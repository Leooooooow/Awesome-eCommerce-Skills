# TikTok Growth

## What this skill solves

Turn product and audience context into a TikTok content system that is more likely to produce useful traction.

## Use when

- The user wants to know what to post on TikTok
- The user needs a TikTok Shop content plan
- The user wants content pillars, hook ideas, or format ideas
- The user wants a content strategy tied to sales outcomes

## Do not use when

- The user wants live trend scraping or automated posting
- The user needs video-level analytics instead of strategy
- The user wants only a script with no strategic context

## Inputs to ask for

Ask only for the minimum needed:
1. Product or category
2. Target audience
3. Account type or TikTok Shop status
4. Main goal: reach, clicks, or conversions
5. Available assets or proof points, if any

## Workflow

1. Clarify product, audience, and goal.
2. Identify the likely purchase triggers and objections.
3. Build 3-5 content pillars.
4. Suggest video types and repeatable formats.
5. Separate reach ideas from conversion ideas.
6. Rank what to test first.

## Output requirements

Always include:
- Content pillars
- Best angles per pillar
- Video or series formats
- Hook directions to test
- Reach vs. conversion balance
- Weekly or batch plan

## Quality standards

- Be specific to TikTok and ecommerce
- Tie recommendations to business outcomes
- Keep advice practical enough to use immediately
- Avoid generic “go viral” language

## Safety / constraints

- Do not promise results
- Do not confuse trend chasing with strategy
- Do not recommend automation when the task is planning
